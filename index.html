Chassis mount design & structural redesign

CHASSIS ENGINEER — FALL 2025 – PRESENT

Contributing to the design and manufacturing of GT Off-Road's tubular space frame for the 2025–26 BAJA SAE vehicle. Focused on chassis mounts that carry axial loads over 800 lbf while cutting subsystem mass versus prior years — deriving load cases in ANSYS Static Structural from cornering, braking, and impact scenarios, validating against hand-calculated force approximations, then manufacturing the mounts on a 4,500 W fiber laser cutter with cutting paths tuned to limit heat-affected zones and hold weld-fit tolerances.

  • Operate and maintain a FabLight FL4500 fiber laser cutter to manufacture precision steel chassis mounts
  • Convert CAD geometry into laser-cutting files, optimizing cutting paths and material layout
  • Run load-path analysis on suspension mounting points to identify high-stress regions
  • Coordinate with suspension and drivetrain teams on mounting interface constraints

SHEET 03 / 05ROBOTICS RESEARCH LAB

Tension-actuated robotic wrist mechanism

UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCHER — FALL 2025 – PRESENT

Designing a 3-axis, 6-DOF tension-based wrist mechanism for docking stabilization and astronaut EVA grasping at the Machine Intelligence and Robotics Laboratory. The tendon-actuated design keeps the end-effector lightweight by mounting servos remotely on the forearm; prototypes are 3D-printed in carbon-fiber-reinforced PLA for stiffness under cyclic tension loads, then mounted to a RIZON robotic arm for grip-angle testing with data processed in MATLAB.

  • Design, test, and prototype a compact tendon-actuated wrist mechanism
  • 3D-print 15+ carbon-fiber parts per prototype iteration
  • Log and model grip-angle test data in MATLAB and Excel
  • Work hands-on with servos and Arduino for actuation

Result: iterating toward a compact 6-DOF mechanism validated on a robotic test arm
